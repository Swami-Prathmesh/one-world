What this is. A client-side reader over open sources: the community iptv-org channel index, public RSS feeds, Project Gutenberg via Gutendex, Open Library, Internet Archive, OpenAlex and NASA. No API keys are stored in this page, no analytics run, and nothing about you is transmitted anywhere except the requests needed to fetch what you asked for. Known limits: a channel being listed is not proof it is licensed in your country; feeds and APIs can rate-limit; and some streams refuse cross-origin playback.
Sources. Headlines come from the publishers' own public RSS feeds, fetched through a CORS relay. Every card links to the original article on the publisher's site and shows the publication time and publisher. Nothing is rewritten or republished here.

How this section works. What these organisations publish openly — articles, event pages, project updates — is fetched live and shown here. Content behind their own gates is not embedded: Prachyam's OTT catalogue is a paid subscription service, and its films, series and masterclasses open on Prachyam's own platform under their terms. Live sessions from The Jaipur Dialogues are carried on third-party video platforms, which this project does not embed. Nothing is copied, mirrored or re-hosted — every card points at the publisher.

Scholarly standard. Every entry names its tradition, source text and reference, and links to an external scholarly edition so you can verify it. Original-language text is given in its own script with a Roman transliteration. English and Hindi renderings here are project translations made for orientation, not authoritative editions — where traditions or translators differ, that is noted. Nothing is invented: entries are limited to passages the project can cite, and this is a starter set meant to grow through verified catalogues, not a complete canon.
